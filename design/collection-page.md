# ALA collection page review

The MXG-ALA product brief v1.1 §3.9.1 and §3.9.6 specify an independent public URL. The homepage and primary navigation use their existing destinations. The demo page is at `/cobranza/` and is also linked contextually from Ayuda and the security advice in Centro de ayuda.

The opening action leads to the three steps for a missed or upcoming payment. A secondary action leads to official support. The reading order then covers what happens around the due date and how to report a problem. FAQ and contact details follow as references. The page retains one H1, the five original topics, and six questions. The amber warning marks the specific case of a personal-account payment request.

[Tala's payment guide](https://talamobile.mx/como-pagar/) and [Baubap's payment guide](https://www.baubap.com/dondepagar) put payment steps and channels ahead of FAQ and other details. The wording and customer-support information follow ALA's demo copy. The hero uses a generated editorial image based on ALA's neighborhood-shop setting. Daylight, a worn notebook, and a naturally patterned cotton shirt set the visual direction. The visual hierarchy uses the shared ALA type scale, spacing, controls, and FAQ component.

The preview carries the demo's search-indexing restriction. The formal ALA site requires indexable article content and the final legal and localization copy per §3.9.2 and §3.9.5. CMS editing and UBS events are production requirements specified by §3.9.3 and §3.9.4.
