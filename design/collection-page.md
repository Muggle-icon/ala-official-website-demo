# ALA collection page review

The MXG-ALA product brief v1.1 §3.9.1 and §3.9.6 specify an independent public URL. The homepage and primary navigation use their existing destinations. The demo page is at `/cobranza/` and is also linked contextually from Ayuda and the security advice in Centro de ayuda.

The opening action leads to the three steps for a missed or upcoming payment. The verification shortcut leads to the official contact section within the same page. The reading order then covers what happens around the due date and how to report a problem. FAQ and contact details follow as references. The page retains one H1, the five original topics, and six questions. The amber warning marks the specific case of a personal-account payment request.

The opening uses the light blue surface from the ALA brand page. The message illustration reads “Verifica primero” on wider screens, and a simplified version sits beside the verification shortcut on phones. The blue verification shortcut links to the official contact section. Below the opening, a fine gray line connects the three numbered actions horizontally on wider screens and vertically on phones. The due-date phases, complaint checklist and warning are paired when space allows. The FAQ uses the shared component with neutral feedback. Inline links keep a thin underline. The official contact section pairs its introduction and support action with the telephone numbers and service hours. On phones the article shows the contact details before the action. The footer follows the homepage’s company, support, legal, and disclosure content.

The preview carries the demo's search-indexing restriction. The formal ALA site requires indexable article content and the final legal and localization copy per §3.9.2 and §3.9.5. CMS editing and UBS events are production requirements specified by §3.9.3 and §3.9.4.
